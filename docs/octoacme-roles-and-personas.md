# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Business Analyst

### Role Summary
Business Analysts clarify requirements, document stakeholder needs, and translate business goals into actionable scope. They bridge the gap between stakeholders and the delivery team to ensure shared understanding of what needs to be built and why.

### Responsibilities
- Elicit, document, and validate business requirements
- Translate stakeholder needs into clear acceptance criteria
- Identify and communicate scope boundaries and trade-offs
- Support backlog refinement and story breakdown
- Facilitate requirements review sessions with stakeholders and delivery teams
- Create and maintain requirement documentation and traceability matrices

### Goals
- Ensure requirements are clear, testable, and aligned with business objectives
- Reduce rework and scope creep through precise definition upfront
- Enable productive collaboration between business and technical teams

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Works closely with Product Managers** on prioritization and business outcomes
- **Partners with Developers and Technical Leads** to validate feasibility and identify implementation gaps
- **Supports Project Managers** by clarifying dependencies and managing scope changes
- **Engages with Stakeholders and Support teams** to capture real-world needs and constraints

### Typical Communication
- Requirements workshops and stakeholder interviews
- Acceptance criteria refinement in backlog planning
- Requirements review and walkthrough sessions
- Documentation in project repository (requirements specs, process maps, data dictionaries)

---

## Technical Lead / Architecture Lead

### Role Summary
Technical Leads guide technical direction, review design tradeoffs, and coordinate with developers and QA on implementation feasibility. They ensure solutions align with system architecture, scalability requirements, and organizational technology standards.

### Responsibilities
- Review and guide architectural decisions and design tradeoffs
- Ensure technical solutions meet quality, security, and performance standards
- Coordinate with developers on implementation approach and technical debt
- Identify and escalate technical risks and dependencies
- Mentor developers and conduct technical design reviews
- Validate integration points and cross-system dependencies

### Goals
- Deliver technically sound solutions that scale and maintain quality
- Reduce technical debt and long-term maintenance burden
- Enable developers to make informed technical decisions autonomously

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Collaborates with Product Managers and Business Analysts** to understand requirements and propose solutions
- **Works with Developers** on design reviews and mentoring
- **Partners with QA teams** to define testability requirements and integration test plans
- **Coordinates with Operations/Release Managers** on deployment considerations
- **Supports Project Managers** by identifying technical risks and mitigating strategies

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and architecture discussions
- Code review feedback on complex or high-risk changes
- Technical risk documentation and mitigation plans
- Design documentation and runbooks

---

## Scrum Master / Delivery Facilitator

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters support team cadence, remove process blockers, and help keep ceremonies effective. They foster a culture of continuous improvement and enable the team to self-organize while maintaining alignment with project goals.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate sprint planning, standups, reviews, and retrospectives
- Help identify and remove impediments to team progress
- Coach the team on agile practices and continuous improvement
- Maintain sprint health and burndown tracking
- Escalate blockers and dependencies to Project Manager
- Support team self-organization and decision-making

### Goals
- Maintain consistent and predictable delivery cadence
- Create psychological safety for feedback and experimentation
- Empower the team to solve problems and improve continuously

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Partners with Project Managers** on schedule coordination and escalation
- **Works with Developers and Technical Leads** to remove technical and process blockers
- **Engages with Product Managers** to clarify priorities and manage scope within sprints
- **Supports all team members** by maintaining effective communication and meeting discipline

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning, daily standups, and retrospectives
- Impediment logs and escalation notes
- Sprint burndown and velocity tracking
- Continuous improvement action items and follow-ups

---

## Operations / Release Manager

### Role Summary
Operations and Release Managers coordinate deployment readiness, execute releases, and verify post-release success. They manage the critical path from build completion to stable production operation, ensuring minimal disruption and rapid recovery if issues occur.

### Responsibilities
- Coordinate deployment planning and release readiness checks
- Manage release execution and deployment automation
- Verify post-deployment health and monitoring setup
- Maintain deployment runbooks and incident response playbooks
- Coordinate with infrastructure and support teams on deployment timing
- Track and communicate release status to stakeholders

### Goals
- Execute releases with minimal risk and downtime
- Ensure rapid detection and response to post-release issues
- Build confidence in release process through documentation and repeatability

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Collaborates with Project Managers** on release timeline and go/no-go decisions
- **Works with Developers and Technical Leads** to validate deployment readiness and rollback procedures
- **Partners with QA teams** to coordinate smoke testing and acceptance verification
- **Engages with Support/Customer Success teams** on customer communication and issue tracking
- **Coordinates with infrastructure/platform teams** on deployment infrastructure and monitoring

### Typical Communication
- Release planning and readiness checklists
- Deployment runbooks and incident playbooks
- Post-deployment verification reports
- Release notes and stakeholder announcements

---

## Support / Customer Success Representative

### Role Summary
Support and Customer Success Representatives share customer-impacting feedback, escalations, and rollout readiness concerns. They serve as the voice of the customer, ensuring the team understands real-world usage patterns and customer pain points.

### Responsibilities
- Gather and communicate customer feedback and pain points
- Escalate customer-impacting issues and bugs
- Assess customer readiness for new features and changes
- Provide input on rollout strategy and communication timing
- Support release announcements and customer documentation
- Track customer adoption and impact metrics post-release

### Goals
- Ensure customer needs are understood and prioritized
- Minimize customer disruption and support burden during releases
- Drive adoption and customer satisfaction with delivered features

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Works with Product Managers** to prioritize feature requests and bug fixes based on customer impact
- **Engages with Project and Delivery teams** on release timing and rollout strategy
- **Supports Operations/Release Managers** with customer communication and early warning signs
- **Partners with Business Analysts** to validate requirements against real customer needs
- **Collaborates with Developers** on documentation clarity and support-friendly features

### Typical Communication
- Customer feedback summaries and impact assessments
- Release readiness and rollout coordination
- Customer communication templates and announcements
- Post-release adoption and satisfaction tracking
